# Jimin Hwang

Hi, I’m Jimin Hwang, and I am a PhD student in the Department of Earth Sciences at the University of Manitoba, Canada.

I am interested in improving our understanding of subsurface systems (especially groundwater) by integrating hydrogeological and geophysical data.

My methodological interests include machine learning, geostatistics, numerical modeling, and inverse modeling.

<br>

I have worked on leveraging machine learning to investigate groundwater dynamics under climate change, including:

- Projection of future groundwater levels under CMIP6 SSP scenarios using explainable deep learning.
- Characterization of regional groundwater drought using unsupervised machine learning.
- Estimation of groundwater–surface water exchange fluxes under hydroclimatic extremes using physics-informed neural networks.


I am currently working on:

- Hydrogeophysics (with a focus on EM methods).
- From characterization to forecasting: developing integrated workflows based on open-source tools (e.g., pyEMU and SimPEG) to combine EM geophysics and groundwater modeling.
