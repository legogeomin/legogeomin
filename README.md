# Jimin Hwang

Hi, I’m Jimin Hwang, and I am a PhD student in the Department of Earth Sciences at the University of Manitoba, Canada.

I am interested in advancing our understanding of groundwater systems through the integration of hydrological and geophysical data.

I have worked on leveraging machine learning to investigate groundwater dynamics under climate change, including:

- Projection of future groundwater levels under CMIP6 SSP scenarios using explainable deep learning. [Link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352801X25001389)
- Characterization of regional groundwater drought using unsupervised machine learning. [Link](https://authors.elsevier.com/c/1o3~u,rVniLK2h)
- Estimation of groundwater–surface water exchange fluxes under hydroclimatic extremes using physics-informed neural networks. [Link](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7545125)


I am currently working on:

- Hydrogeophysics (with a focus on EM methods).
